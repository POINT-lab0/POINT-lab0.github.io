Join & Contact Us

The POSTECH Interaction Laboratory (POINT) is constantly seeking highly motivated students and researchers who are eager to push the boundaries of haptics, human-computer interaction (HCI), virtual reality (VR), robotics, and multisensory perception. At POINT, you will conduct cutting-edge research, collaborate with world-class international institutions and industry partners, and contribute to technologies that reshape how humans experience the digital and physical worlds.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome talented researchers with a strong publication record in haptics, HCI, VR/AR, robotics, or related fields. Postdoctoral fellows at POINT are given substantial research autonomy and opportunities to lead high-impact interdisciplinary projects and grant proposals.

Graduate Students M.S. / Ph.D. / Integrated Ph.D.

We are looking for passionate graduate students with solid backgrounds in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Cognitive Science, or related disciplines. Students with a strong drive for research excellence, problem-solving skills, and a solid foundation in programming or mathematics are encouraged to apply.

Undergraduate Research Assistants Interns

We offer undergraduate research participation opportunities for motivated students interested in gaining hands-on experience in research. Interns work closely with senior researchers and graduate students on active lab projects.

How to Apply

If you are interested in joining POINT, please contact Prof. Seungmoon Choi via email (choism@postech.ac.kr) with the following documents:

  • CV Curriculum Vitae — including education, research experience, and publication list if applicable
  • Transcript Academic Transcripts — for student applicants
  • SoP Statement of Purpose — explaining your background, research interests, and motivation for joining POINT

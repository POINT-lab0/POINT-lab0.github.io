Interaction Lab

We are an interdisciplinary research group dedicated to understanding and designing haptic interaction — the science and engineering of touch-based feedback between humans and computers.

Our work spans haptic rendering & perception, multisensory interaction in XR, wearable & assistive haptic devices, and novel haptic interfaces. By combining human perception studies with cutting-edge hardware and software, we create immersive experiences that feel as natural as the real world.

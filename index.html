POSTECH Interaction Laboratory

The POSTECH Interaction Laboratory (POINT) pursues easy, effective, and novel methods for human-system interaction, with a primary focus on physical and multisensory experiences across computers, robots, smart vehicles, and mobile devices. Our research is inherently interdisciplinary, integrating haptics, human-computer interaction (HCI), virtual reality (VR), robotics, and perceptual psychology. Recognized globally for our pioneering contributions to touch-based, immersive interfaces and multisensory perception, POINT strives to advance the fundamental science of physical interaction while bridging the gap between human capabilities and technology.

POINT was established by Prof. Seungmoon Choi in 2005 as the Haptics and Virtual Reality Laboratory. In 2020, the lab was renamed the Interaction Laboratory to reflect our broadened research vision and growing impact on next-generation interactive systems.
